## BEGIN STITCH PROMPT

### Role & deliverable

You are generating **UI prototype mockups** (wireframe-to-high-fidelity layouts) for a **personal homepage website**, not production code.

**Deliverables:**

- Separate prototype frames/screens for **Home**, **About Me**, **Contact Me**, and **Works** (portfolio).
- Show **consistent header/navigation** across screens.
- **Do not embed real photographs or illustrated portraits.** Use neutral **generic placeholders only** for any avatar/portrait slot (e.g., simple circle silhouette icon, initials block, or “Avatar” labeled empty frame).
- Prefer **vectors, typography, geometric dividers**, and labeled empty states over stock imagery.

### Style reference (Designprompts.dev spirit)

Treat the aesthetic as influenced by prompts from **[Design Prompts](https://www.designprompts.dev/)**—specifically traits common to curated dark UI styles such as **Minimal Dark**, **Luxury**, and strong **Bold Typography**:

- Editorial restraint: lots of negative space, grid discipline, crisp alignment.
- High-contrast dark foundation with **premium metallic accent** touches (below).
- Confident typography hierarchy; modern sans-serif (or restrained geometric sans); avoid noisy decoration.

Overall: **Black minimalist**, luxury editorial, understated and precise.

### Brand & IA

**Site intent:** Personal homepage + showcase of works/projects.

**Logo wordmark:** `TONY` (all caps) as primary brand lockup.

**Global navigation (top-right, persistent):**

- Home
- About Me
- Contact Me
- Works

**Interaction note (annotate on prototype):** Clicking nav items triggers **page-to-page transitions** described below.

---

### Screen requirements

#### 1) Home

- Logo/wordmark **`TONY`** is prominent (placement can be editorial—e.g., large hero typography or anchored top-left, but navigation remains **fixed top-right**).
- Maintain **black minimalist** composition.
- Indicate this is the landing/intro zone without cramming dense content.

#### 2) About Me

- In the primary content focal area, show the phrase **`HELLO WORLD`** as the dominant headline (large, commanding).
- Supporting layout can remain minimal/subtle secondary text blocks as placeholders only.

#### 3) Contact Me

- Include an **avatar area** represented as **placeholder only** (no face photos).
- Include a **GitHub link** prominently in the contact cluster (button or text link styling consistent with gold accent rules).

#### 4) Works (Portfolio)

- Dedicated projects listing/grid area (cards are fine).
- Keep visuals abstract: **no project screenshots unless shown as blank frames** labeled “Thumbnail placeholder”.

---

### Navigation & layout rules

- **Navbar position:** **Fixed at the top-right** of the viewport (sticky behavior implied).
- **Navigation items:** exactly the four routes listed above.
- **Transition behavior (prototype annotation):**
  - **Page changes:** subtle **fade in / fade out** between routes.
  - **Scrolling effects:** explicitly **none** (no parallax, no sticky section theatrics beyond the fixed nav).

---

### Loading state (critical)

Prototype should include **one standalone loading-screen frame** (or an overlay variant) illustrating an **“old TV set”** loading motif:

- Example cues: CRT frame silhouette, subtle scanlines/curvature cues (stylized, not noisy), monochrome static burst **or** a simple faux “warming up” bar—**still aligned with black/gold luxury minimalism** (avoid kitschy clipart; keep graphic and refined).

---

### Color system & typography cues

**Palette**

- **Backgrounds:** deep black / near-black panels.
- **Primary accent:** bright **gold** (metallic/brilliant highlight sparingly—for key lines, underline rules, hover states, icons).
- **Supporting palette:** restrained dark grays only where needed for separation.

**Text treatment**

- **Headlines / emphasis:** Implement a **gradient text effect from black‑tones into gold** *where readability allows* OR interpret as dark charcoal → gold shimmer on hover—**maintain AAA contrast decisions** on body copy (use gradient primarily on large display type, keep normal text high-contrast).

**General:** Gold is **accent**, not filler—small precise strokes beat large gold blocks.

---

### Non-goals / constraints recap

- **No real avatar images**, no illustrated characters meant to resemble a specific person—placeholders only.
- **No gratuitous scrolling storytelling** animations.
- Prototype-focused: clarity of structure beats decorative assets.

---

### Output formatting request to Stitch

- Provide **labeled frames** per page plus the **loading** concept.
- Include short **sticky notes/callouts** on frames for transitions (fade) and navigation behavior.
- Keep everything cohesive as one **personal brand system** keyed to **`TONY`**.
